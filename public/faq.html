FAQ

Questions about private video meetings

Straight answers about encryption, privacy, limits and how BinaryBond works.

Privacy and encryption

Is BinaryBond really private?

Yes. Meetings are encrypted end to end with a key that is created in your browser and never sent to our server. Audio, video, chat and files travel directly between participants, so the server has nothing readable to store, search or hand over.

What can the BinaryBond server see?

It sees an opaque meeting ID, how many people are connected and when, and the IP address of each connection while it is open. It cannot see names, messages, files, audio, video or the meeting key.

How does end-to-end encryption work here?

The meeting key sits in the part of the link after the # symbol, which browsers never send to servers. Your browser derives a meeting ID, an access token and an encryption key from it. The handshake between devices is sealed with AES-256-GCM, and media is protected with DTLS-SRTP.

How can I check that nobody is intercepting my call?

Open the safety number for another participant and read the four groups of digits aloud. If you both see the same numbers, your connection is not being intercepted.

Does BinaryBond store or record my meetings?

No. Nothing is recorded or kept on the server, and a meeting disappears when the last person leaves. Participants can record only if everyone in the meeting agrees, and the file is saved on the recording person's own device.

Does BinaryBond use cookies or analytics?

No. There are no cookies, no analytics and no scripts or fonts loaded from other companies.

Using BinaryBond

Do I need an account or an app?

No. BinaryBond runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile. Open the meeting link, enter a name and join.

How many people can join a meeting?

Up to 4 on the free tier and up to 8 on the Business plan. Every participant connects directly to every other participant, which keeps calls private but limits very large meetings.

Can I control who joins?

Yes. Turn on the waiting room to approve each person, lock the meeting once everyone has arrived, remove a participant at any time, or schedule the meeting to end automatically.

Can I meet without sharing a link?

Yes. On the Join page, choose Passphrase. Everyone enters the same meeting name and passphrase, and the key is derived on each device. Nothing needs to be sent.

Networks and hosting

Will other participants see my IP address?

In a direct call, yes, as with any peer-to-peer service. If relay mode is available, turn on Hide IP addresses before you start and everyone's media passes through an encrypted relay instead.

Why does a call sometimes fail to connect?

Strict firewalls can block direct connections. Use the Test my connection button in the lobby to see what your network allows, and ask the host to enable relay mode if your network blocks UDP.

Can I run BinaryBond on my own server?

Yes. The project includes a Docker setup with automatic HTTPS and a TURN relay, plus settings for your own limits and branding. See the developer documentation for details.

Can I add BinaryBond to my own app?

Yes. Create a session token on your server and mount the call with the embed SDK. You get webhooks, usage reporting and white-label branding on the Business plan.
