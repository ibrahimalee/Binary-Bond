Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026. This page explains what BinaryBond handles, what it keeps and what it never receives.

On this page Summary What we never receive What we handle What we keep IP addresses Third parties Your device Your rights

Summary

Meetings on BinaryBond do not require an account. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and we do not load code from other companies. Meeting content travels directly between participants' devices and is encrypted end to end.

What we never receive

  • Audio, video and screen shares
  • Chat messages, polls, whiteboard drawings and files
  • Display names, which are encrypted with the meeting key
  • The meeting key or the full meeting link
  • Connection details such as session descriptions and network candidates

What we handle while you are connected

  • A meeting ID derived one way from the meeting key, and a hash of a membership token
  • Encrypted handshake messages, which are passed on and discarded immediately
  • Your IP address, held in memory for the length of the connection for rate limiting

What we keep

  • Daily totals: meetings created, participant minutes and largest meeting size. These are not linked to meetings, people or addresses.
  • For developer workspaces: name, contact email, a hash of the API key, plan, branding, webhook URL and allowed sites.

IP addresses and direct connections

In a direct call, the other participants' devices can see your IP address, as with any peer-to-peer application. If you turn on relay mode, your audio and video pass through a relay server that only handles encrypted packets, and other participants cannot see your address.

Third parties

Your browser may contact a STUN server to find a direct route between devices. Operators can run their own so no outside party is involved. If a workspace buys a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages and is subject to Stripe's privacy policy.

Data on your device

Your settings, such as name, devices and theme, are saved in your browser. The current meeting key is kept in session storage and disappears when the tab closes. The panic shortcut (Shift + Esc) deletes all of it immediately.

Your rights

Because people joining meetings do not have accounts and we do not store personal data about them, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Developer workspaces can be deleted at any time on request.
