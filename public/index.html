How it works

  1. 1

    Your browser creates a secret

    A random 128-bit key is generated locally and placed after the # in your invite link.

  2. 2

    Keys are derived, not shared

    From that secret your browser derives a room ID, an access token and an encryption key. The server only ever sees the first two, and stores only a hash of the token.

  3. 3

    Devices connect directly

    Encrypted handshakes are relayed once. After that, audio, video, chat and files travel peer-to-peer over DTLS-SRTP.

What the server knows

  • An opaque room ID
  • How many people are connected
  • When the room opened and closed

What it can never see

  • Your name, messages or files
  • Audio and video
  • The room key or invite link
  • Connection fingerprints
