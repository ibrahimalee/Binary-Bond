Private by design

Only the people in the meeting can see or hear it

The meeting key is created in your browser and travels inside the invite link, in a part of the address that browsers never send to a server. Our server passes along encrypted handshakes and nothing else.

  • Calls connect device to device over DTLS-SRTP
  • Compare a safety number to rule out interception
  • Optional relay mode hides your IP address from other participants

How it works

From link to call in seconds

  1. Start a meeting

    Your browser creates a random key and puts it in the meeting link. It never reaches our server.

  2. Share the link

    Send it however you like. Turn on the waiting room if you want to approve each person.

  3. Talk directly

    After a short encrypted handshake, audio, video, chat and files flow straight between devices.

Common questions

More answers on the full FAQ.

Do I need an account?

No. Click Start a meeting, share the link, and you are in. Accounts exist only for developers who embed BinaryBond in their own products.

Can BinaryBond see or record my meetings?

No. The key that protects a meeting is created in your browser and never sent to us. Our server only helps devices find each other, and the messages it passes along are encrypted with that key.

How many people can join?

Up to 4 on the free tier and up to 8 on the Business plan. Every participant connects directly to every other participant, which keeps calls private but makes very large meetings impractical.

Will other participants see my IP address?

In a direct call, yes, as with any peer-to-peer app. Turn on relay mode before joining and your connection goes through an encrypted relay so others cannot see it.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge on desktop and mobile, and you can add it to your home screen.

Can I run it on my own server?

Yes. BinaryBond ships with a Docker setup that includes automatic HTTPS and a TURN relay. The developer docs cover the details.
