When BinaryBond is the right choice

It is built for small, sensitive conversations where privacy matters more than audience size.

  • Client, patient, legal and financial conversations
  • Team meetings where recordings should not sit with a third party
  • Journalists and sources who need to protect their identities
  • Products that want to offer encrypted calls without handling the media themselves
