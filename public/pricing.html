Pricing questions

Is the free meeting tier really free?

Yes. Personal meetings do not require an account, and there are no ads or data sales funding them.

What happens if I go over my minutes?

Calls already in progress continue. New meetings created with your API key are paused until the next month or until you upgrade. You get webhook warnings at 80% and 100%.

Can I self-host instead?

Yes. The server is open for self-hosting with Docker. You can configure your own plans and limits.

How do I cancel?

From the billing portal in your dashboard, at any time. Your workspace moves back to the Developer plan.
